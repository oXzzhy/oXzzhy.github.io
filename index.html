My name is Haoyu Zheng and I am a student learning Economics. I would like to know how individuals make choices and how it impacts businesses and society. Through studying Economics, I can observe things in different ways in daily life. I also wish to know more about competition and how businesses adapt to changes in consumers’ demand. All of these can relate to what we have learned in class to real-life situations and thus Economics can be applied to individual decisions and business decisions.
